## What this case draws from it

1. The June 27, 2024 House Energy & Commerce markup of the American Privacy
   Rights Act was cancelled amid House GOP leadership vowing behind closed
   doors to block the bill "whether it was approved by the committee or not"
   — corroborating this case's independently-sourced Johnson/Scalise account
   (E13).
2. The bill's final-week revisions stripped civil-rights and
   algorithmic-decision-audit provisions in order to court conservative
   Republicans.
3. By the week of the cancellation, APRA had "lost the support of dozens of
   major privacy and civil liberties groups" — the article names the ACLU,
   Center for Democracy & Technology, NAACP, Japanese American Citizens
   League, Autistic Self Advocacy Network, Asian Americans Advancing Justice,
   Access Now, Demand Progress, and Free Press Action.
