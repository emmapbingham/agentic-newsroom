## Hypothesis

Rep. Andy Barr (R-KY), chair of the House Financial Services Subcommittee on
Financial Institutions and now GOP nominee for Kentucky's open Senate seat,
runs a documented influence loop with America's Credit Unions (formerly CUNA,
merged with NAFCU 2024): his press releases introducing CFPB-curbing bills
(Rectifying UDAAP Act, TABS Act) quote the association's endorsement; the
association's LD-203 filings report $40,000 FECA honoring him 2022–2025; and
the association's giving pivoted to "Andy Barr for Senate, Inc." dated the
very day Mitch McConnell announced his retirement (2025-02-20).

## Why it's newsworthy

The member who writes the credit-union lobby's top regulatory priority into
bills — and quotes their applause in his own press releases — is about to
become a senator, and the same lobby's own compliance filings show its money
following him into the open-seat race from day one, literally. Every link of
the loop (endorsement, legislation, contribution, timing) is in public
records with dates. CFPB's fate is a live 2025–26 fight, and McConnell's seat
is one of the cycle's marquee races.

## What would confirm it / what would kill it

- Confirms: the 2025-02-20 item verified on the LD-203 filing image + FEC
  records for the committee redesignation; the full financial-sector honoree
  stack showing ACU among the earliest to the Senate committee; ACU lobbying
  activity descriptions naming his bills; bill/markup dates interleaving with
  contribution dates across cycles.
- Refutes / boring: (a) the 2025-02-20 date is a reporting artifact (check
  date vs attribution; filer error) — verify on the filing itself; (b) ACU
  PAC gave the same way to many Financial Services members (base-rate check:
  ACU's full honoree footprint — is Barr top-tier or median?); (c) the
  endorsement-quote practice is uniform across all his financial bills, not
  ACU-specific; (d) $46k over 4 years is modest by financial-sector PAC
  standards — scale check against his other honoree registrants.

## Verdict

**Closed — folded into `acu-legislative-bench` as its Barr chapter.**
Builder→skeptic→judge ran on E1–E9: every factual link verified (skeptic
re-derived all numbers exactly), but the Barr-centric framing itself is
refuted — the timing hook is dead (the Senate committee is a redesignation
collecting industry money from 2025-01-31, ACU only ~11th by dollars), the
"loop" is not unique to Barr (ACU runs the identical endorse→lobby→pay
triple with ≥5 other members, three now senators), and ACU itself was a
pipeline selection artifact (ABA gave Barr more and endorsed the same bills
but is invisible to the mention screen). The judge's recommended path — that
the skeptic's kill IS the story, one level up, as the association's
documented legislative bench — was accepted by the editor (Emma) and
promoted as lead `acu-legislative-bench` on 2026-07-07. This case's E1–E9 are
imported unchanged into that case's evidence.md as `EBarr1`–`EBarr9`; this
file stays as the standalone record of how the Barr-only framing was tested
and why it didn't hold on its own.
